// lesson_1 · model_1

Model 1: a square without repeat

1 2

1 the motor letter 2 its turn direction arrow

Pick the motor that turns your car the right way. Wrong motor or wrong direction: your car spins the wrong way.

  • One side is a move block, set to seconds.
  • One corner is a Motors block that runs ONE of your drive motors on its own.
  • Slower turns tidier. set movement speed to 50 % before you start.
  • Mark your square with pencils: 2 pencils for each side.

// lesson_1 · model_2

Model 2: the same square with repeat

Build the side and the corner once, then repeat them. How many repeats make a square? That is yours to work out.

  • The side and the corner sit inside the repeat loop together.
  • The loop is the same code as Model 1, built once.
  • Test one side and one corner first. Then repeat them.

// brain_break

Four Corners

hands off the cars   No talking while you choose.

  • Each corner of the room has a card suit symbol: Spades, Hearts, Diamonds, Clubs.
  • Everyone quietly chooses a corner to stand in.
  • The teacher draws a card, either from a deck of cards, or by scanning the QR code to use an online card drawing app.
  • The teacher announces the suit on the card (for example “Clubs!”).
  • Everyone standing in that corner is out and must sit down.
  • The rest of the students choose a new corner for the next round.
  • Keep playing until only one student is left: they are the winner!

Back in your seat when the timer ends. Cars stay where they are.

Mr Igor draws the card here.

Build your own course

▶ Challenge 2: Coding (your Doc) ▶ Challenge 2 Support - Coding tutorials ▶ the SPIKE online app On your own, or with a partner.

  • Your roles: the ringed box on your sheet. On your own, you are both. In a pair, one each, and you can swap.
  • Lay out a course on the floor using pencils.
  • Code one robot, or two robots, to follow your course from start to finish and stop where it should. It can go in a loop.
  • Use degrees to turn at angles that are not right angles.
  • You can take it outside if Mr Igor says yes.
  • Test. Change one thing. Test again.
  • Show Mr Igor when it finishes where it should.
